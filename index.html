Group Chat Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Group Chat is a private messaging app for members of an invite-only group. Ryan Martin runs the app and its server as a personal project. Questions: support@ryanjmartin.net.

What we collect

  • Account details: your display name, username, and the phone number or email address you sign in with. Passwords are stored only in hashed form.
  • What you send: messages, photos, videos, voice messages, files, reactions and polls. These are stored on our server so the group can read them. Chats are not end-to-end encrypted by default, so the server operator can technically access them.
  • Location: only when you choose to share it in a chat. It's sent as a message.
  • Activity in the app: read receipts, typing indicators and online status, which are shown to other members.
  • Device details: a push notification token, and the names and last-used times of your signed-in devices.

The app uses your camera and microphone only when you take a photo, record a voice message or join a call. The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking.

How we use it

Only to run the group chat: to sign you in, deliver messages, send notifications, keep the service secure, and relay messages to members who take part by text message. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Other members of your rooms see what you post there, along with your display name.
  • Telnyx delivers text messages. Sign-in codes sent by text, and group messages exchanged with members on SMS, pass through Telnyx and the phone carriers.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications.
  • Cloudflare carries network traffic to the server.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Account details and messages are kept until the account or message is deleted. Text-message consent records are kept as needed to show compliance with messaging rules.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open Settings > Account and choose to delete your account. This deactivates the account and removes your sign-in details. You can also request deletion by emailing support@ryanjmartin.net from the address or with the phone number on your account; we'll respond within 30 days.

Messages you already sent stay in the rooms where other members can see them. To have specific messages or files removed, delete them in the app or ask us by email.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (TLS). No system is perfectly secure, so please don't share anything highly sensitive in the group.

Children

Group Chat is for people 18 and older, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
